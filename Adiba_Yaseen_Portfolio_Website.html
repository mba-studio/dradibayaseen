01 · Profile

Research with real-world evaluation in mind.

Adiba’s work connects machine learning, bioinformatics, and molecular data. Her research moves from structural protein analysis to peptide safety, compound–protein screening, and graph neural networks for targeted inhibition.

Her projects emphasize reliable evidence: similarity-controlled splits, nonredundant cross-validation, independent external datasets, experimentally verified negatives, and ranking measures designed for large screening libraries.

Alongside research, she has taught artificial intelligence, machine learning, programming, object-oriented programming, databases, operating systems, networks, and information technology. She is positioned for university faculty and AI research opportunities in Riyadh and across Saudi Arabia.

02 · Selected research

Evidence-led biomedical AI.

Four connected case studies show the progression from classical structural features to contextual embeddings, screening-oriented metrics, and graph-based molecular learning.

Therapeutic peptides
Published · 2021

HemoNet: predicting peptide hemolytic activity

A neural framework combining contextual SeqVec peptide embeddings with SMILES-derived fingerprints for terminal modifications. The model supports L- and D-amino acids and evaluates generalization under stringent nonredundant and external tests.

Compound–protein interaction evaluation

Designed realistic evaluation for CPI prediction by controlling train–test similarity, negative construction, and screening metrics. RFPP translates model output into the number of compounds screened before finding a known interaction.
